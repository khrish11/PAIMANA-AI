## Data Sufficiency Assessment

### 2025-2026 Data Alone
- The current dataset covers 13 reporting periods (July 2025 - July 2026)
- This is insufficient for most projects to reach completion
- Most projects are ongoing with <100% progress

### Historical OCMS Data
- Historical OCMS data may provide additional completed projects
- Would extend the time horizon for project completions
- Recommended for RCF if available

### Conclusion

The 2025-2026 PAIMANA data alone is **NOT sufficient** for reliable RCF.
Historical OCMS data or longer time horizon is required for most
reference classes to meet the 15-project minimum requirement.
